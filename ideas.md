# CrimDev design brief

## Direction
**Reference:** Steam-inspired storefront density and discovery patterns, reinterpreted as an original dark neon indie game hub. The site should feel familiar to PC players without copying Steam's logo, copy, or exact visual assets.

## Design system
- **Movement:** dark editorial game storefront with cyan/teal structure and coral/orange discovery accents.
- **Principles:** high information density, obvious clickable game cards, generous cover art, strong hierarchy, and readable utility copy.
- **Color philosophy:** charcoal and blue-black surfaces; mint/cyan for active navigation and metadata; warm orange for calls to action and sale-style emphasis.
- **Layout:** fixed-width responsive shell, top utility bar, hero feature strip, horizontal/stacked catalog sections, reusable detail template.
- **Signature elements:** CRIMDEV wordmark, slashed C mark, pill genre tags, compact stat rows, blue-gray content panels, and a soft glow only behind key art.
- **Interaction:** hover lift on cards, clear focus states, route-like links through query-string game IDs, and instant upload previews on the submit form.
- **Animation:** restrained 180ms transitions; no looping motion that competes with game art.
- **Typography:** system sans for utility and body copy; wide uppercase display treatment for brand and labels.
- **Brand essence:** a practical, creator-friendly game shelf built for adding games through GitHub.
- **Voice:** direct, slightly gritty, welcoming to small teams.
- **Wordmark:** CRIMDEV in a condensed, bold uppercase treatment with a slash-mark icon.
- **Signature color:** electric cyan `#66c7d9`, balanced by ember orange `#ef7a4d`.
